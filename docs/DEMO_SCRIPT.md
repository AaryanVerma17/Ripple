# 3-minute demo

1. **Overview** – "Rover Alpha: speed 1.88 m/s, runtime 1.81 h, 5.74 kg, $243. The map shows how parts depend on each other."
2. **Analyse** – click "Motor → 24 V compact". Show three plans: A keeps the 12 V rail (motor underpowered), B raises it (driver, regulator, battery fail with arithmetic), C swaps parts to fix it: **$297 vs the $260 budget** → one verified issue left. Open a finding's calculation and evidence.
3. **Ripple map** – the controller is not affected, with the reason.
4. **Missing data** – supply the wiring ratings with a source; the unknowns resolve on re-run.
5. **Camera** – upload a motor photo; Gemini identifies it; price compared; "Analyse this change".
6. **Chat** – "What if the chassis is 0.5 kg lighter?" then "What does it cost?" and "What are my options?".
7. **Review** – record a decision as the engineer; export Markdown. Close: "Ripple never approves. It shows its work."
